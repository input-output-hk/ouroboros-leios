The prototype separates concerns into three stores plus the logic that moves transactions between them:

1. **The Mempool** (`Ouroboros.Consensus.Mempool.*`) — the Praos mempool, extended with a Leios tx-hash index, a no-cache snapshot path for the forge, and time-bounded re-application.
2. **The LeiosTxCache** (`LeiosTxCache*`) — a bounded **in-memory index** over recently-announced EBs, their bodies, and the acquisition/validation state of the txs they reference. *An index, not a store*: the bytes live in the LeiosDb.
3. **The LeiosDb** (`LeiosDemoDb*`) — the on-disk (SQLite) store of EB points, bodies, and tx bytes, split volatile/immutable with reference-counting GC.

`LeiosDemoLogic` orchestrates fetch/serve; `LeiosVoting` consumes the cache to vote cheaply; the forge (`NodeKernel/Forge.hs`) drains the mempool into RB+EB payloads. (The module names carry a `Demo` prefix throughout — prototype-era naming that survived into the staged code.)

### 1.2 The forge path: `partitionMempool`

[`o-c-d/Ouroboros/Consensus/NodeKernel/Forge.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/src/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/Ouroboros/Consensus/NodeKernel/Forge.hs#L700) — `partitionMempool` returns `(rbTxs, ebTxs, rbTxsSize, MempoolSnapshot, Maybe (LeiosCert, EbHash))` and is the implementation of the formal spec's abstract `splitTxs`:

- **`decideLeiosCertify`** first determines whether this block certifies the previously announced EB (quorum reached in `LeiosVoteState`).
- **TxRB branch** (no cert): one `getSnapshotFor` snapshot; `snapshotTake snap rbCap` fills the RB, then `snapshotTake snap (rbCap ⊕ ebCap)` minus the RB prefix fills the **announced EB from the overflow** — where `ebCap = ebCapacityTxMeasure` (ImpactAnalysis **NEW-LeiosEbCapacityMeasure**).
- **CertRB branch**: the RB carries only the certificate; `resolveAndApplyLeiosClosure` applies the certified EB's closure onto the parent state (`LeiosClosureApplied{lcaStateAfterEB, lcaClosureDiff}`), the result is ticked and diff-composed (`prependDiffs`), and the mempool is **rebased** through `getSnapshotForNoCache` against parent ⊕ closure ⊕ tick before filling the fresh EB.
- Era-level forging: `forgeLeiosEb :: SlotNo -> NonEmpty (Tx TopTx era) -> ForgedLeiosEb` in [`o-c-c/shelley/Ouroboros/Consensus/Shelley/Ledger/Forge.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-cardano/src/shelley/Ouroboros/Consensus/Shelley/Ledger/Forge.hs); the HFC threads `Maybe ForgedLeiosEb` through [`HardFork/Combinator/Forging.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/HardFork/Combinator/Forging.hs).

### 1.3 Designed but not landed at this snapshot

The [design document's mempool chapter](https://github.com/input-output-hk/ouroboros-leios/blob/11be71594ef1d71db7917ced11ccd54795335c07/docs/leios-design/README.md#high-throughput-mempool) specifies three mechanisms; the branch at `b56977b` contains none of them as such ❓ (grep-verified for the first; the section's own `TODO: Not yet implemented` warnings cover the others):

- **Double-buffered mempool** — on-screen committed buffer vs off-screen `Sync` with a converging delta loop and `Remove` preemption. Benchmarked by Sebastian Nagel via devnet experiments ([ouroboros-leios#911](https://github.com/input-output-hk/ouroboros-leios/issues/911), [#845](https://github.com/input-output-hk/ouroboros-leios/issues/845): ~400 tx/s ingest; the deep-mempool throughput *inversion* finding), status "the data structures and function names below track the ouroboros-consensus implementation and may evolve" — staging visible in the `bladyjoker/leios-prototype/optimistic-mempool` branches on node and consensus. However, `implSyncWithLedger` at `b56977b` *does* already contain a converging catch-up loop with a bounded under-lock residual (`syncDeltaCap = 256` / `syncMaxIters = 8`) — the sync-side half of the converging-delta idea, without the on-/off-screen buffer pair.
- **Cheap ticking** — mempool-level validity `interval` making slot advance an intersection test.
- **Ready-to-forge views** — two pre-computed views (certify vs not), optimistic reads, capped reconciliation. `reapplyUntilTimeout` (§ 1.1) is the landed *cap* half.

The design doc records a striking motivation: on a 12-node devnet, a mempool of ~8 EBs of capacity **inverted** load-vs-throughput (2.5× ingest ⇒ −33% on-chain) versus a ~2.5-EB mempool, entirely in the tail of forge-preparation cost — "the objective is therefore not a small Mempool but a cheap large one."

## 2. The LeiosTxCache

### 2.1 Role and invariants

[`o-c/LeiosTxCache.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosTxCache.hs) (the umbrella; its haddock is the authoritative design note): an **in-memory index of the LeiosDb** recording, per tracked tx, whether it is *acquired* and whether it is *validated* — "by the Mempool or by the LeiosVoting thread." It supplants the by-hash membership check that fetch logic would otherwise pay disk latency for. Invariants (quoted from the module): an indexed announcement/body/tx **is in the LeiosDb and pinned**; DB insert happens *before* index insert; ChainDB evicts from the index **before** pruning the DB. The one tolerated race: an index hit followed by a failed DB read implies the *announcement itself* was pruned, so the reader can soundly short-circuit (orphaned EB).

### 2.2 The handle and the index

[`LeiosTxCache/API.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosTxCache/API.hs) — `data LeiosTxCache m a v b` with operations:

- `insertAnnouncement :: SlotNo -> RbHash -> EbHash -> m (Set EbHash, Set TxHash)` — count-driven eviction cascades out of the returned sets; capped by `maxAnnouncementCount = 128` (annotated `TODO magic number`).
- `evictOlderThan :: SlotNo -> m (Set EbHash, Set TxHash)` — tip-driven eviction; carries the **ORDERING CONTRACT** (index before DB, "never after, never concurrently… reverse the order and you arm the hit-prune hazard: a false hit ⇒ a skipped fetch ⇒ a silently-incomplete EB closure"). Wired to the ChainDB's GC in [`Storage/ChainDB/Impl/Args.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Storage/ChainDB/Impl/Args.hs#L100) (mandatory argument, no default).
- `insertBody :: EbHash -> b -> w -> (w -> Int -> TxHash -> BytesSize -> w) -> m (Maybe (LeiosTxCacheInsertBodySummary, w))` — registers a held body, bumps per-tx refcounts, and **in the same pass folds the caller's accumulator over the not-yet-acquired references** (the misses), returning the funnel summary.
- `withLockedInsertUnappliedTx` / `withLockedInsertAppliedTx` / `withLookupTx` — exclusive-write / read-locked accessors; unapplied inserts are bucketed by `TxArrivalPrior` (`TxWasUntracked` / `TxWasNotYetInserted` / `TxWasAlreadyHeld`) into `FetchArrivalBytes` observability counters via `bucketTxArrival`.
- `class ReferencesTxsByHash b where foldTxReferences …` — the body abstraction; production instance decodes `SerializedEbBody`.

[`LeiosTxCache/Reference.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosTxCache/Reference.hs) — the pure index:

```haskell
data LeiosTxCacheIndex a v b = MkLeiosTxCacheIndex
  { announcementState :: Map SlotNo (NEMap RbHash EbHash)  -- retained announcements
  , announcementCount :: Int                                -- ≤ maxAnnouncementCount
  , bodyState         :: Map EbHash (BodyState b)           -- refcount = #announcements
  , txState           :: Map TxHash (TxState a v)           -- refcount = #inserted bodies
  , prunedSlot        :: SlotNo }                           -- monotone eviction watermark

data TxState a v = TxNotYetInserted RefCount
                 | TxAlreadyInserted RefCount a   -- fetched, unapplied
                 | TxAlreadyValidated RefCount v  -- applied (Mempool/LeiosVoting)
data BodyState b = BodyNotYetInserted RefCount | BodyAlreadyInserted RefCount b
newtype RefCount = MkRefCount Word8   -- INVARIANT > 0
```

[`Optimized.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosTxCache/Optimized.hs) (`newHashTableLeiosTxCache`) is the production handle. Its design (from the module haddocks): the **~2M-entry tx map** lives in a [`MutableHashTable`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosTxCache/Optimized/MutableHashTable.hs) — salted open addressing (SipHash-2-4), linear probing with **backward-shift (tombstone-free) deletion**, a separate occupancy bitset, "a Haskell port of the reference `hash_table.c`". Keys are 32 bytes as four unpacked `Word64`s (no per-key heap object); the value packs the tx's **refcount and 2-bit state tag into one `Word64`** (hence only `a = v = ()` is supported); the backing store is a `MutableByteArray` the GC accounts for but never traces or copies. The small announcement/body state stays in ordinary `Map`s (`data HtState b`) behind an `MVar` that also serializes hash-table access. Quality gates: **observational-equivalence properties against the pure reference** (`prop_equiv`, `prop_equivEvict` in [`Test/LeiosTxCache/Optimized.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/test/consensus-test/Test/LeiosTxCache/Optimized.hs)), a [`leios-txcache-bounds-checked`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/test/leios-txcache-bounds-checked/Main.hs) suite recompiled with `-fcheck-prim-bounds` so out-of-bounds byte-array access fails loudly, an internal `checkInvariants`, and a `bench/leios-txcache-bench`. Worst case: `worstCaseCacheTxCount = maxAnnouncementCount × maxTxsPerEb` — the fixed denominator of the funnel's `ibsCacheLoad`.

**Divergence from the written design worth noting:** ImpactAnalysis **NEW-LeiosTxCache** specified an LRU cache of *all* recent txs (mempool entrants included, ~1-hour age, priority queues + double-buffered mmapped file); the implementation is instead an **announcement-windowed refcount index over EB-referenced txs only**, with byte storage delegated to the LeiosDb. The funnel telemetry's panel note ("tracked reaching txsInEb would mean the mempool had failed to have them") reflects the implemented semantics.

## 4. The flows that tie them together

### 4.1 EB body arrival — `processLeiosBlock` ([`LeiosDemoLogic.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosDemoLogic.hs#L869))

1. Validate size/hash/duplicate-tx-hash (peer-sourced only; forged EBs skip).
2. `leiosDbInsertEbPoint` + `leiosDbInsertEbBody` (DB before index, per invariant), then `insertBody` into the cache, folding out the **misses** as `IntMap offset → (TxHash, BytesSize)`.
3. **Mempool pull**: look up the *full* tx set (not just cache misses) in the mempool's Leios index — `mkMempoolPull` over `getLeiosTxIndex` with an era-specific to-bytes conversion; `noMempoolPull` for forge/tests. Partition into `mempoolNotCache` (full ingest: DB + cache-Applied), `mempoolAndCache` (mark Applied only — already persisted), and `missedBoth = misses ∩ notInMempool` (**the actual fetch set**).
4. Emit `TraceLeiosBodyHits point txCacheSummary (size mempoolHits) (size missedBoth)` — combined hit rate = (txsInEb − missedBoth)/txsInEb, the dashboard's headline number.
5. `Jobs.mkLeiosJobPool` over `missedBoth` (bounded by `maxJobBytesSize`/`maxJobTxCount` from `LeiosFetchStaticEnv` — still the demo env, tagged TODO) → LeiosFetch clients pull tx batches from peers; mempool-found txs are ingested synchronously last.
6. If the announcement was already evicted (cache insert no-op), a **backstop** classifies txs via `withLookupTx` directly and the arrival is bucketed `fetchArrivalEvicted`.

An arriving fetched tx enters through `withLockedInsertUnappliedTx` (arrival classified by `TxArrivalPrior`); completing an EB's tx set fires `TraceLeiosBlockTxsAcquired` and DB `missingTxCount` transitions; **offer gating** realizes the CIP's serve-when-secured rule at two levels: the LeiosDb emits `AcquiredEb` on body insert and `AcquiredEbTxs` only when the closure completes (idempotent completion trigger in [`LeiosDemoDb/InMemory.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosDemoDb/InMemory.hs#L198); SQLite analogue), and the notify server's pump maps these to `MsgLeiosBlockOffer` / `MsgLeiosBlockTxsOffer` per peer ([`NodeToNode.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/src/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/Ouroboros/Consensus/Network/NodeToNode.hs#L605)) — so the *body offer* goes out on body acquisition while *tx-serving* is gated on completion.

### 4.2 Voting — `validateEbClosure` ([`LeiosVoting.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosVoting.hs#L416))

Load the closure from the LeiosDb (`resolveLeiosClosure`), resolve input UTxOs, then per tx `decide` via `withLookupTx`: a cache hit tagged validated licenses **`reapplyTx`** (static checks skipped) via `assumeValidatedClosureTx` — "the tag is the evidence… the token is built here and nowhere else"; otherwise full **`applyTx`**. This is the CIP's set-S validation-flag reuse ([§ EB transaction handling](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/CIPs/blob/master/CIP-0164/README.md#eb-transaction-handling)) and ImpactAnalysis's third validation level. Voting timing is wall-clock-stubbed: the window opens `lHdrWait = 3 s` after the announced slot's onset and closes `lVoteWindow = 4 s` later, with the deadline checked before and after closure validation and both constants carrying TODOs to become ledger protocol parameters ([`LeiosVoting.hs:140`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosVoting.hs#L140)); voting further requires the selected tip to still be the announcing RB and a committee seat. Verdict type `EbClosureVerdict` (`EbClosureInvalid !(ApplyTxErr blk)` on failure).

### 4.3 CertRB adoption — `ResolveLeiosBlock` ([`Storage/LedgerDB/Forker.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Storage/LedgerDB/Forker.hs#L767))

```haskell
class ResolveLeiosBlock blk where
  resolveLeiosClosure :: Monad m => LeiosDbConnection m -> EbHash -> m [(TxHash, GenTx blk)]
  leiosTxHashOfGenTx  :: GenTx blk -> Maybe TxHash
```

A Dijkstra-era **CertRB carries no regular transactions on the wire — its body holds the Leios certificate** (`mkBody`: tx sequence `Seq.empty`, certificate written via `leiosCertBlockBodyL` — [`Shelley/Ledger/Forge.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-cardano/src/shelley/Ouroboros/Consensus/Shelley/Ledger/Forge.hs#L130-L144)); `resolveAndApplyLeiosClosure` splices the EB closure from the LeiosDb into validation (`applyLeiosClosure` onto the unticked parent, then `tickThenApply` the CertRB), "mirroring how a non-CertRB Praos block's body txs would be applied — but sourced from the LeiosDB." Era instances in [`o-c-c/shelley/Ouroboros/Consensus/Shelley/Ledger/Leios.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-cardano/src/shelley/Ouroboros/Consensus/Shelley/Ledger/Leios.hs) (`deserialiseLeiosTx`; the tx hash is over exactly the serialized bytes, matching `forgeLeiosEb`). Mempool `Sync` then removes the adopted txs — ordinary Praos-style revalidation, bounded by `implSyncWithLedger`'s converging off-lock catch-up loop (`syncDeltaCap = 256` residual reapplied under lock, `syncMaxIters = 8` safety valve — [`Update.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Mempool/Update.hs#L492)); `reapplyUntilTimeout` instead bounds *snapshot* computation (`computeSnapshot`, [`Query.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Mempool/Query.hs#L143)). The staging behavior is **landed at this snapshot**, not just design intent (**NEW-LeiosCertRbStagingArea** / **UPD-LeiosRbBlockFetchClient**): `isUnacquiredCertRB` / `ignoreUnacquiredCertRB(Suc)` transiently hide a CertRB (and its successors) from candidate construction while the predecessor's announced closure is absent ([`ChainSel.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Storage/ChainDB/Impl/ChainSel.hs#L1557), applied in `constructPreferableCandidates`), and `leiosAcquiredEbsRunner` enqueues `chainSelReprocessLeiosEb` when the closure's `AcquiredEbTxs` notification fires ([`Background.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Storage/ChainDB/Impl/Background.hs#L154)).

### 4.5 Announcements, equivocation, and fetch jobs

- **Announcements** ([`LeiosDemoLogic/Announcements.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosDemoLogic/Announcements.hs)): an EB announcement *is* the RB header's optional (EbBody hash, size) field — adjacent to the election proof, signed by the electee, so **every announcement has a slot/age**, which bounds in-memory announcements to 0/1/2 per sufficiently-young election (none / unequivocal / **equivocal**). The ≤ 2-per-election limit is enforced *per upstream peer* by `PeerState.live :: Map ElId (ElState anc)` (`OneAnnouncement`/`TwoAnnouncements`, `ElId = MkElId SlotNo poolColdKeyHash` — [`Announcements.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosDemoLogic/Announcements.hs#L52-L149)); a third announcement (or a repeat) throws and **disconnects** that peer. [`ElBimap`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosDemoLogic/Announcements/ElBimap.hs) is the *downstream relay gate*: it records which peers received a first announcement so the second (equivocation evidence) is relayed only to them. Relay is urgent but best-effort — the per-peer enqueue is dropped when that peer has no free LeiosNotify credit (CIP budget L_hdr ≈ 1 s; ~hundreds of outstanding `MsgLeiosNotificationRequestNext` credits per connection); validation ([`Announcements/Validate.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosDemoLogic/Announcements/Validate.hs), `validateAnnouncementHeader`/`AnnouncementInvalidity`) runs **protocol-level header rules plus applicable envelope limits** (`additionalEnvelopeChecks`, e.g., maximum header size), forecast from the immutable tip — the chain-extension envelope check is deliberately skipped since announcements arrive out of order; syncing nodes never request notifications.
- **Fetch jobs** ([`LeiosDemoTypes/LeiosJobs.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosDemoTypes/LeiosJobs.hs)): `missedBoth` is partitioned greedily into `LeiosJob`s inside a per-EB `LeiosJobPool` (least-requested selection, multiplicity bookkeeping, demo defaults `maxJobBytesSize = 64 KiB`, `maxJobTxCount = 20000` from `LeiosFetchStaticEnv`). Request priority is two-tiered (`fetchPriorityTiers`): EBs within L = 3·L_hdr + L_vote + L_diff of the current slot are requested **oldest-first**, staler EBs then freshest-first; with the slot unknown (syncing) everything lands in the first tier; each job commits to a `JobRootHash` over its covered tx hashes **so a response can be validated without the EB body**.

### 4.6 Votes to certificate

[`LeiosVoteState.hs`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/LeiosVoteState.hs): `data LeiosVoteState m = { addVote :: LeiosVote -> m AddVoteResult, subscribeVotes, queryCert :: RbHash -> m (Maybe LeiosCert) }` — votes are validated (`validateLeiosVote`), deduplicated, tallied per announcing-RB point, and the certificate is **assembled eagerly the moment the tally crosses the quorum threshold** (`aggregateLeiosCert`; `AddVoteResult = NoCommittee | VoteInvalid | AlreadyKnown | Added VoteTally (Maybe LeiosCert)`, with `VoteTally {vtWeight, vtTally, vtThreshold}` carried as a record precisely so telemetry can't transpose weights). A `FIXME: Garbage collection of vote state` stands — the design doc's ~10-minute vote retention (**NEW-LeiosVoteStorage**) is not yet implemented. At forge time, [`decideLeiosCertify`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/src/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/Ouroboros/Consensus/NodeKernel/Forge.hs#L300) reads the tip header's announcement from the protocol state (`protocolStateLeiosAnnouncement`), enforces the ledger-configured **minimum certification gap** — ⌈(3·L_hdr + L_vote + L_diff)/slotLength⌉ over the millisecond-valued Dijkstra parameters; musashi's genesis fetched 2026-09-17 yields **14 slots**, while Aug-2026 Slack said 10 (unreconciled ❓🤖; see [the parameter note](./leios-node-protocol-parameters.md) § 5) — as a *minimum wait* — `currentSlot − announcementSlot ≤ minGap ⇒ Nothing` — so a successor RB elected *inside* the gap cannot certify the pending EB, which is what strands EBs (the kleioscan 1 − 0.95^g skip expectation models exactly this under an f = 0.05 active-slot assumption). Enforcement is **forge-side only** at this snapshot: follower block validation does not check the gap ([`Forker.hs:618`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Storage/LedgerDB/Forker.hs#L618) `FIXME`). The forge then requires the closure to be present in the LeiosDb, and only then queries the certificate — "EB certification must happen within one block (this is the linear aspect of linear Leios)."
