- **Lifecycle-audit implementation facts** (verified by source reading during the 2026-09-17 accuracy review of the [transaction-lifecycle diagram](./artifacts/leios-node-tx-lifecycle.svg); layer: implementation @ ouroboros-consensus `b56977b`):
  - The **minimum certification gap is a minimum wait** (`currentSlot − announcementSlot ≤ minGap ⇒ Nothing` in `decideLeiosCertify`), and certification must occur in the announcing RB's direct successor — so a successor RB elected *inside* the gap strands the pending EB. Enforcement is **forge-side only**: follower validation carries `FIXME: Check the min certification gap` (`Forker.hs:618`), with no other validation-side use of `getMinCertificationGap`.
  - A **CertRB's wire body carries the Leios certificate and no regular transactions** (`mkBody`: tx sequence `Seq.empty`, certificate via `leiosCertBlockBodyL`); "empty body" comments are shorthand.
  - **Missing-closure CertRB parking is implemented**: `ignoreUnacquiredCertRB`/`...Suc` transiently hide a CertRB and its successors from chain-selection candidates until the closure arrives; `leiosAcquiredEbsRunner` + `chainSelReprocessLeiosEb` reprocess on `AcquiredEbTxs`.
  - **Fetch priority is two-tiered** (`fetchPriorityTiers`): EBs within L = 3·L_hdr + L_vote + L_diff of the current slot are requested oldest-first, staler EBs then freshest-first; slot unknown (syncing) ⇒ one oldest-first tier.
  - **Vote timing is wall-clock-stubbed**: window opens at announced-slot onset + `lHdrWait = 3 s`, deadline + `lVoteWindow = 4 s` later (both TODO-marked to become ledger protocol parameters); the check runs immediately if the closure completes after the window opens.
  - The ≤ 2-announcements-per-election limit is enforced per upstream peer by `PeerState`/`ElState` (third or repeat ⇒ disconnect); `ElBimap` is the downstream relay gate for equivocation evidence.
  - **Offer gating is two-level**: LeiosDb emits `AcquiredEb` on body insert (→ `MsgLeiosBlockOffer`) and `AcquiredEbTxs` only on closure completion (→ `MsgLeiosBlockTxsOffer`); a completed SQLite row stores `missingTxCount = −1` as the notified sentinel.
  - Mempool `Sync` is bounded by a **converging off-lock catch-up loop** (`syncDeltaCap = 256` under-lock target, `syncMaxIters = 8` safety valve); `reapplyUntilTimeout` bounds *snapshot* computation, not `Sync`. Default mempool capacity is 2× the ordinary block measure, byte-overridable; the CIP's ≥ 2 × (RB+EB) formula awaits `ouroboros-consensus#2280`.
  - Demo fetch-job caps: `maxJobBytesSize = 64 KiB`, `maxJobTxCount = 20000`. LeiosDb EB statuses are not one linear chain: certified 0→1→2, cleanup 0→3 and 2→3, and promotion rescues 3→1.
  - Two source comments are **stale at this very commit**: ChainSync announcement folding *is* wired (`leiosMsgRollForwardCallback` → `processAnnouncementCentrally ReceivedViaChainSync`), and "proper EB announcements not implemented" reduces to body-size validation still trusting request/offer-carried state.

## Protocol Parameters

- **The Leios parameter set is nine Dijkstra ledger protocol parameters** (verified 2026-09-17 by source reading at cardano-ledger `1587f21`, the node's exact build pin; layer: implementation): `leiosAnnouncementPeriodLength`/`leiosVotePeriodLength`/`leiosDiffusionPeriodLength` (Milliseconds32 — L_hdr/L_vote/L_diff), `leiosCommitteeSize` (Word16, N_c), `leiosQuorumStakeThreshold` (UnitInterval, τ), `maxEndorserBlockReferencesSize`/`maxEndorserBlockTxsSize` (Word32 bytes), `maxEndorserBlockExUnits`, `maxRefScriptSizePerEndorserBlock` — all on-chain-governable, all in the NetworkGroup/SecurityGroup update groups. `maxKeyAge` for voting keys is deliberately *derived* (⌈KES lifetime in epochs⌉ + 2), not a parameter.
- **Deployed musashi values** ([`dijkstra-genesis.json`](https://book.play.dev.cardano.org/environments-pre/leios/dijkstra-genesis.json), [`shelley-genesis.json`](https://book.play.dev.cardano.org/environments-pre/leios/shelley-genesis.json), and [`config.json`](https://book.play.dev.cardano.org/environments-pre/leios/config.json) fetched 2026-09-17; layer: deployed network — **weekly redeploys, re-fetch before use**): L_hdr/L_vote/L_diff = 1000/4000/7000 ms; N_c = 900; τ = 0.75; the 100 kB EB-reference parameter becomes a count ceiling of 2,777 transactions but does not itself guarantee an encoded reference list ≤ 100 kB; closure ≤ 1 MB; EB ExUnits 310 M mem / 100 G steps; ref-scripts ≤ 4 MB; slot 1 s, epoch 21,600 slots, f = 0.05, k = 108. The node requests `MempoolCapacityBytesOverride` = 2,000,000 B, which rounds to a 2,072,576 B effective byte coordinate at the 90,112 B block capacity. **Derived min certification gap = ⌈14,000 ms / 1 s⌉ = 14 slots**, conflicting with the Aug-2026 Slack claim of 10 ❓🤖 (unreconciled — weekly redeploys may have changed the periods, or "10" conflated the `fetchPriorityWindowSlots` constant, whose own comment says "~14 s on mainnet, ~10 on the testnet"). File hashes and configuration genesis hashes are recorded in [the parameter note](./artifacts/leios-node-protocol-parameters.md#sources).
- **Quorum mechanics** (layer: implementation @ pins above): seat weight = pool's share of total active stake (`Weight = Rational`, committee weights sum ≤ 1); vote acceptance tallies `totalW ≥ threshold` with re-votes replacing; `verifyLeiosCert` enforces bitfield well-formedness, rejects any signer bit on a keyless seat, requires `Σ seatWeight ≥ τ`, and verifies one aggregate BLS12-381 min-sig signature — matching CIP-0164's `Σ stake(votes) ≥ τ·stake_total-active`. Committee = **top-N_c pools by stake, deterministic** (CIP-0164 "stake-based truncation"; weighted Fait Accompli with Local Sortition (wFA^LS) is explicitly the earlier-drafts scheme), seated by the ledger SNAP rule on the mark snapshot for epoch e+1; keys honored while `epoch < registeredIn + maxKeyAge`.
- **Enforcement asymmetry** (layer: implementation; the sharpest finding): the honest forge precisely enforces transaction bytes, execution units, and reference-script bytes, but reduces `maxEndorserBlockReferencesSize` to a transaction-count ceiling using the minimum 36 B encoded entry; the count ceiling does not guarantee the actual encoded reference list satisfies the byte limit. The forge-side certification gap in `decideLeiosCertify` and the voter's stubbed 3 s/7 s window are implemented, but the Byzantine-facing acceptance checks are FIXME/TODO — no ledger rule consumes the EB-cap parameters, `Forker.hs:618` skips the gap, and `Praos.hs:526` TODO-lists five unimplemented header checks (`LeiosCertRBWithoutAnnouncement`, `LeiosCertTooYoung`, `LeiosEbTooBig`, `LeiosEbTxsTooBig`, `LeiosEbCertExclusivity`). The one fully-enforced acceptance rule is the certificate's quorum + signature verification. Votes are not epoch-tagged (TODO) and certs are not committee-tagged (FIXME). Wire clamping: governance can raise EB caps past the 500 kB / 12 MB wire constants and "nothing rejects that today." Details: [the protocol-parameter note](./artifacts/leios-node-protocol-parameters.md).
- **The forge's certification gap is one slot stricter than CIP-0164** (verified 2026-09-17 by source reading; layer: implementation @ consensus `b56977b` vs CIP `d07a30b`). The CIP admits a certificate when `slot(RB′) − slot(RB) ≥ gap`; `decideLeiosCertify` guards `elapsed ≤ minGap ⇒ Nothing` ([`Forge.hs:322`](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/ouroboros-consensus/blob/b56977baae0740f563060a8a9171c78be865b357/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/src/ouroboros-consensus-diffusion/Ouroboros/Consensus/NodeKernel/Forge.hs#L322)), so it certifies only at `≥ gap + 1`. The EB point carries the announcing RB's slot, so the comparison is like-for-like. Consequence for skip-rate modeling: the at-risk window is `gap` slots under the implementation (1 − 0.95¹⁴ ≈ 51.2% stranded at f = 0.05) but `gap − 1` under the CIP (≈ 48.7%). **Caution:** the CIP quotes 0.95¹³ ≈ 51% for no intervening RB, hence EB survival under that model; that is the complement of the ≈51% skip figure in our notes. Not recorded in source as deliberate ❓🤖.
- **Two CIP-0164 vote conditions are absent from the prototype voter, while a third is enforced incidentally outside voting** (verified 2026-09-17; layer: implementation @ `b56977b`): (1) "the RB header arrived within L_hdr" is not checked for voting; announcement age separately controls 5-minute relay and 10-minute receive guards and supplies body/closure telemetry, but neither guard compares with L_hdr. (2) "no equivocating RB header for the slot" is detected per peer by `PeerState`/`ElState` (and the peer disconnected), but `AnnouncementEquivocation` is consumed only for tracing, `goVote` never consults it, and `LeiosNotVotedReason` has no such constructor, even though `lHdrWait` exists as the observation window. (6) "the EB contains at least one transaction" is not a vote rule; it is enforced incidentally because `leiosDbInsertEbBody` calls `error "empty EB body (programmer error)"`, reached on the per-peer LeiosFetch thread, so an empty peer EB drops that peer. Conditions 3 (deadline, from stubs), 4 (`tipAnnouncerFor`) and 5 (`validateEbClosure`) are enforced by the voter.
- **Received votes face no timing check** (verified 2026-09-17; layer: implementation @ `b56977b`): `LeiosVoteState.addVote` checks dedup, committee seat, key and signature only, and `LeiosVote` carries `(announcingRbHash, voterId, voteSignature)` — no slot, no timestamp — so the voting window cannot be checked at ingest without a chain lookup. The window is honest-node self-restraint; a tally can cross τ from late votes.
- **Stale-comment findings from this pass** (layer: implementation): `Validate.hs`'s claim that Dijkstra header rules "already fold in … the EbBody size bound" is contradicted by the `Praos.hs:526` TODO at the same commit; `selectCommitteeByStake`'s haddock ("cumulative stake reaches σ_c") mismatches its code (counts seats, not stake) — the ledger's `selectLeiosCommittee` is the production path and does count seats as documented.
