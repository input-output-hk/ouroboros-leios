### Diagrammed the timing inequalities, and found two divergences from the CIP 🤖

Drew [artifacts/leios-timing-inequalities.svg](../artifacts/leios-timing-inequalities.svg): a slot-indexed timeline of every timing rule governing EB voting and certification, in three tiers — the CIP-0164 design inequalities over the network characteristics (Δ_hdr, Δ_EB^O, Δ_EB^W, Δ_RB, Δ_reapply, Δ_applyTxs), the deployed period parameters composing the 14-slot gap, and an eleven-row enforcement table keyed to CIP-0164's own numbered protocol flow. Boxes are coded by enforcement status rather than by subsystem, which is the part the CIP's Figure 4 cannot show.

Checking the CIP's rules one at a time (rather than reading the timing code on its own terms) surfaced two findings, both now in [facts.md](../facts.md) and § 7 of [the parameter note](../artifacts/leios-node-protocol-parameters.md): (1) **the forge's gap guard is one slot stricter than the CIP** — `elapsed ≤ minGap ⇒ Nothing` rejects the CIP's own boundary slot, which also means the stranding window is `gap` slots (≈ 51.2%) rather than `gap − 1` (≈ 48.7%), and incidentally that the CIP's "≈51%" is a *survival* probability where ours is a *skip* probability; (2) **three of the CIP's six vote conditions are unenforced** — header-arrival-within-L_hdr is telemetry only, equivocation is detected and traced but never consulted by `goVote`, and the non-empty-EB rule is honored only incidentally by an `error` labeled "programmer error" that an empty peer EB can reach (dropping that peer). A fourth gap sits outside the CIP's list: received votes get no timing check, and `LeiosVote` carries no slot or timestamp for one to be expressible.

> [!WARNING]
> Correction, 2026-09-17: two vote conditions are absent from the voter; the nonempty-EB condition is enforced incidentally during database ingestion, not absent from the prototype. Header arrival is not checked against L_hdr for voting, but announcement age is not telemetry-only: separate five-minute relay and ten-minute receive guards apply. The CIP's ≈51% is specifically the probability of no intervening RB, hence survival under that model.
